FDM PRINTING

Fused Deposition Modeling

FDM is the most versatile 3D printing technology, perfect for functional parts, large-scale prints, and rapid prototyping. Our fleet of professional FDM printers handles everything from small detailed components to large architectural models.

  • Build volume up to 400 × 400 × 500mm
  • Layer resolution from 0.05mm to 0.3mm
  • Over 8 material options including PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU, ASA, PC
  • Infill from 10% to 100%
  • Sanding, priming, and painting post-processing available

RESIN / SLA

Stereolithography

SLA resin printing delivers exceptional surface quality and detail resolution for demanding applications like jewelry, dental models, miniatures, and fine art reproduction. When standard FDM resolution isn't enough, SLA delivers.

  • Layer resolution as fine as 0.025mm
  • Ultra-smooth surface finish, minimal post-processing
  • Standard, ABS-like, flexible, and castable resins available
  • Ideal for display models, jewelry patterns, and miniatures
  • UV curing and washing included in service

SLS NYLON

Selective Laser Sintering

SLS printing with nylon powder offers unparalleled design freedom — no support structures needed, allowing for complex geometries, interlocking assemblies, and internal channels impossible with other methods. Industrial strength, professional results.

  • No support structures required — maximum design freedom
  • Excellent mechanical properties — strong, flexible, heat-resistant
  • Nylon PA12, glass-filled nylon, TPU powder options
  • Ideal for functional assemblies, hinges, snaps, and living parts
  • Natural, dyed, or custom color finishing available
